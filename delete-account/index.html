Railroad Mandate

You can delete your Railroad Mandate account, and everything stored with it, at any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open the game and sign in.
  2. Tap Company, then Leaderboard, then My account under The World.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then tap it again to confirm.

Your account is deleted at once.

By email

Write to support@railroadmandate.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Your email address, your player name, your sign-in details and all your scores on the world leaderboard. Nothing is kept afterwards. Games saved on your own device stay there until you delete the game or its data.
