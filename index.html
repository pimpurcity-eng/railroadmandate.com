Your first run, stop by stop

You never have to guess what to do next. A NEXT button at the top of the screen shows the next stop on the line, with a cash reward when you reach it.

  1. 1

    Ride your first train

    You start with one line and one engine. Follow it out of town, or climb into the cab.

    +$1,000
  2. 2

    Deliver a job

    Towns post jobs: forty tonnes of grain here, flour there. Deliver on time and your record goes up.

    +$3,000
  3. 3

    Open a mine or a farm

    Coal, iron, timber and grain sit outside every town. Build the works and your trains carry what they dig and grow.

    +$5,000
  4. 4

    Build a factory, then go inside

    Enlarge the building from the outside, then fit machines on the open floor. Each level you buy shows up as new machinery.

    +$5,000
  5. 5

    Research better engines

    Thirteen fields, from locomotive engineering to shipping and ports. Steam gives way to diesel, electric and high speed.

    +$6,000
  6. 6

    Win a government contract

    Governments need coal, steel and munitions moved. Bid low enough to win, and deliver what you promised.

    +$8,000
  7. 7

    Go public, then take over a rival

    List your company, buy your rivals' shares, and when you hold enough, their railway becomes yours.

    +$25,000

When the country goes to war, it calls you

You don't fight the wars. The governments do. But armies run on railways, and someone has to haul the shells, the coal and the troops to the front.

War brings rich government contracts and cut lines that need repair. Peace brings the boom.
