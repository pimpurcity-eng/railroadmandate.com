Railroad Mandate

Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

This policy covers the Railroad Mandate game on Android, Windows and in the web browser. In short: you can play without an account and nothing about you leaves your device. If you make a free account for the world leaderboard, we keep only your email, your player name and your game scores. The game shows no ads, and we never sell or share anything about you.

What the game keeps on your device

Your company, your progress, your settings and your own records of past games are saved on your own device. They stay there, and you can remove them at any time by deleting the game or its data.

Your account (optional)

A free account puts your games on the world leaderboard. When you make one we keep:

  • Your email address, to sign you in, to confirm the address once and to send a password-reset link when you ask for one. It is never shown to other players.
  • Your player name, which you choose and can change. It is shown on the leaderboard.
  • Your game scores: for each game, your company's name, the region, the year it reached, and its money made, business earnings, company value, freight and passenger figures. These are shown on the leaderboard with your player name.
  • If you choose Sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address so we can sign you in. We do not get your Google password.

Your password is never stored by the game; the account service keeps only a secure hash of it. On your device the game keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in; signing out deletes it.

Accounts are stored with our database provider, Supabase (servers in Canada), only to run the game's accounts and leaderboards.

Deleting your account

In the game: Company, Leaderboard, My account, then Delete my account. Your account, player name and scores are deleted at once and for good. You can also ask us by email: see how to delete your account.

Google Play Games (Android)

Some Android versions offer Google Play Games sign-in. If you use it, Google tells the game your Play Games name and player ID, and may keep a backup of your company as a Play Games saved game in your own Google account, under Google's own privacy policy. Google Play Games also collects some diagnostic information about its own service; see what Play Games collects.

What we do not do

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or device identifiers. The game has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
